03 / PROCESS

Retention is designed in the cut.

Every decision—shot length, image order, narration sync and reveal timing—serves one clear goal: keep the story moving without losing clarity.

01

Find the visual question

I identify the image, sound or unanswered detail that can stop a viewer in the first seconds.

02

Build the narrative spine

I map the hook, context, escalation and reveal before polishing transitions or effects.

03

Cut to the voice

Visual changes follow the narration’s meaning and energy—not a fixed three-second template.

04

Finish for mobile

I check focal points, sound clarity, compression and pacing in the final 9:16 frame.
